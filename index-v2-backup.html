What your site is costing you — Trek Las Vegas Kayaking Tours & Rentals —

I'm Jarrett, a Las Vegas web designer. I went through your site the way someone planning an Emerald Cave kayak trip would: find the tour, compare the options, book. The foundation is strong: your pages have their own titles and canonical addresses, your markup carries your name, phone and address, the reviews are on the page, and the search score was 100 on every page I tested. The leaks are a homepage that is slow to respond on a phone, a Book Now button with too little contrast, a street address in every header and footer that isn't where your tours start, and a www address that doesn't load.

Checked October 17, 2026

What I found

01

Your homepage is slow to respond on a phone.

In a simulated slow-connection load test on a phone-size screen, the homepage scored 46 and 60 for speed. The main content showed at 12.1 and 3.6 seconds, and the page was blocked from responding for 440 and 2,520 milliseconds. The page transferred 2,224 KiB; the embedded YouTube player alone was about 1,037 KiB. The half-day tour page scored 49 and its main content showed at 4.6 seconds. Two runs on the homepage, one on each other page; field speed is unmeasured.

02

Your Book Now button is hard to read.

White text on your blue (#2c9ec2) measures 3.09 to 1, and the standard asks for 4.5 to 1 at this size. The test flagged it on the header's “BOOK NOW” button and on the tour buttons, and the same blue on white for small links. Accessibility otherwise scored 96.

03

The address on every page isn't where your tours start.

The header and footer of every page I opened give 704 Canyon Rd #15, Boulder City, NV, 89005. Your Emerald Cave tour pages list the meeting point as Willow Beach Marina, 25804 N Willow Beach Rd, Willow Beach, Arizona 86445, and say all tours are self-drive with no shuttle. Your Full Moon Night Paddle lists Boulder Beach, Lakeshore Rd, Boulder City, Nevada 89005. A guest who types the address from your footer into a map, or a search tool that quotes it, would go to Boulder City.

04

Your www address doesn't load.

https://www.trekvegas.com/ redirects to itself over and over until the browser gives up (I followed six redirects and stopped). https://trekvegas.com/ sends visitors to your site, but trekvegas.com/about, /contact and your tour pages return “Not Found” instead of forwarding to the matching page on emeraldcavelasvegas.com. Anyone using the trekvegas.com address you email from would hit that.

05

The half-day tour page shifts while it loads.

On the Emerald Cave half-day tour page, the test found 15 layout shifts and a layout-shift score of 0.103, above the 0.1 mark the standard calls good. Pages shifting under a thumb tends to cost taps on the wrong button.

What's working

All 15 pages I opened have a title and a canonical address, 14 have one clear page heading, and the search score was 100 on every page I tested. Your markup names Trek Las Vegas with its phone number and address, the homepage shows 346 verified reviews, and tour prices and durations are on the page. You publish a sitemap, your robots.txt allows every crawler, and an AI Start Here page exists at /llm-start-here.

Unmeasured

Visitor numbers, rankings, the real speed on a customer's phone, whether booking works end to end (I didn't book anything), and your Google and Yelp listings as those sites show them. I didn't call or text anyone.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt allows every crawler and lists the sitemap. I tested 11 well-known AI and search crawler names and none is blocked.

  • Found
    Business markup

    The homepage has TravelAgency and LocalBusiness markup with your name, phone (702) 291 8505 and address, plus review, product and FAQ markup on tour pages.

  • Found
    Page text is readable without scripts

    The homepage HTML carries about 5,400 words and one clear heading, so a crawler that doesn't run JavaScript can still read it.

  • Found
    Social profiles linked

    Your markup links your Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube profiles.

  • Gap
